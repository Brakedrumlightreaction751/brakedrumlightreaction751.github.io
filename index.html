🖥️ What You Need

PHP-Storm is a lightweight desktop utility, so most modern Windows computers handle it comfortably. Before you begin, confirm the following:

  • A Windows desktop or laptop (Windows 10 or Windows 11 recommended).
  • Roughly 1 GB of free disk space for the application and its working files.
  • At least 4 GB of memory, with 8 GB giving you a smoother experience on larger projects.
  • A screen resolution of 1280 × 720 or higher so panels and toolbars display correctly.
  • A stable internet connection for the initial download.
  • An account with permission to install software on the computer you are using.

If your computer meets these everyday standards, you are ready to proceed.

🧭 Your First Session

Once PHP-Storm opens, the screen may look busy at first glance. Here is a simple path through it:

  1. Look at the left side. This column lists the folders and files of the project you have open.
  2. Open a file. Click any file name and its contents appear in the large central area.
  3. Close a tab. Tabs across the top of the central area represent open files. Click the small cross on a tab to close it.
  4. Find something. A search feature is available from the menu bar for locating words or file names.
  5. Adjust the view. Panels can be resized by dragging the thin line between them, and hidden entirely from the menu bar.

Nothing you do in this first session can break anything. Explore freely — the configuration is designed to be reset easily if you change your mind about a setting.
